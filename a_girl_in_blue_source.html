01 — The Ballad

Blue Looks Good on You

I met you today, just a moment ago,
A face I had never seen, a smile I didn't know.
Yet somewhere between hello and the passing of time,
You became the inspiration behind these lines.

It's funny how life has its own little ways,
Of making an ordinary moment stand out from the days.
No grand introduction, no story rehearsed,
Just a simple encounter that inspired a verse.

I won't pretend that I know you by heart,
We've only just met; this is merely the start.
But there's something quite lovely about the way you appear,
A kind of quiet beauty that makes the world feel less severe.

Perhaps it's your smile, or the light in your eyes,
Or the effortless charm you may not even realise.
Perhaps it's the way you carry yourself without trying,
Like a melody playing softly while the whole world keeps moving by.

And no, I'm not saying you've stolen my heart,
Let's not give a new meeting a dramatic start.
I'm simply a man who appreciates what he sees,
And today, that appreciation came quite naturally.

Now, let's talk about blue.

The colour of oceans, of twilight skies,
Of peaceful evenings and beautiful surprises.
A colour that speaks without making a sound,
The kind of quiet magic that's difficult to write down.

You told me it's your favourite, and somehow I smiled,
Thinking how fitting it was, even for a little while.
Because blue has a calmness, a beauty of its own,
And perhaps that's why it feels different now that I've known you.

I used to think blue was just another shade,
One among the countless colours the world had made.
But now, when the evening sky takes on that hue,
I might remember this day—and the girl who loves blue.

Not because I'm making promises beneath the moon,
Or imagining forever after meeting you so soon.
But because some little details have a beautiful way,
Of making an ordinary memory worth keeping someday.

And then there's the music.

The Afrobeats kind, the rhythm that flows,
The kind that lifts your spirit wherever it goes.
A little Burna, some Wizkid in the air,
A melody, a moment, and good vibes to share.

Perhaps one day we'll exchange a song or two,
And I'll ask which Afrobeats track sounds most like you.
You might laugh at my choices, or question my taste,
But at least we'll have a moment neither of us had to waste.

Maybe you'll recommend a song I haven't heard,
And I'll remember the melody more than the words.
Maybe we'll talk about music until the evening grows late,
Or simply exchange playlists—no pressure, no fate.

There's no hurry to the story.

Because getting to know someone shouldn't feel like a race,
There's beauty in allowing things to happen at their own pace.
No need for big promises or words that sound profound,
Sometimes the nicest connections begin with simply being around.

So here's a little ballad, wrapped in midnight blue,
A few honest words inspired by meeting you.
Not a declaration of love, not a question you must answer,
Just a thoughtful little gesture with a touch of romance and laughter.

If this poem makes you smile, then it's done what I intended,
A small piece of today's story, sweetly written and sent.
And if you read it again with a smile you can't explain,
I'll consider that a lovely little bonus to my day.

So keep being yourself; there's no need to do more,
You don't have to be extraordinary to be worth writing for.
And if our paths cross again, as they sometimes do,
I'll be glad to say hello to the girl who loves blue.

Until then, let your music play and your good days multiply,
May you find little reasons to smile beneath every sky.
And should a certain poem ever cross your mind,
Remember, it was written by someone who thought meeting you
was a rather lovely part of his time.

No expectations, no hurried story to pursue—
Just a little midnight poetry, written in blue. 💙

— Mr Reaper
